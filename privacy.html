Privacy Policy

Channel Stats Lab. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Channel Stats Lab ("the Lab") is a private tool that the owner of one YouTube channel runs on their own computer. It is used only by that owner, for that one channel. This policy explains what it does with information.

YouTube API Services

The Lab uses YouTube API Services. Google's own handling of information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

What the Lab accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, they allow the Lab to:

  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (read only);
  • read the channel's YouTube account data, such as its list of videos and their public statistics (read only);
  • upload new videos to the channel as private.

What it collects and stores

  • Statistics and analytics about the channel's own videos: views, engaged views, watch time, how much of each video was watched, likes, comments counts, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources and search terms, and impressions.
  • Each video's title, id, length and publish date.
  • The sign-in token Google issues, so the Lab can keep reading the numbers.

All of this is stored only in files on the owner's own computer. The Lab has no server, and stores nothing anywhere else. It does not collect information about the channel's viewers as individuals: YouTube only gives it totals.

How it is used

Only to show the owner how the channel's videos perform, to help plan new videos, and to upload the owner's own finished videos as private. The owner publishes every video themselves.

Sharing

The Lab never sells, shares or sends this information to anyone. There are no other internal or external parties, no advertising and no third-party content.

Cookies and storage on your device

The Lab uses no cookies. It stores its data and sign-in token in files on the owner's computer, as described above. This website uses no cookies or tracking of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. The owner can also disconnect from within the Lab, which revokes the sign-in at once.

Deleting stored data

If access is revoked from within the Lab, all stored YouTube data is deleted within 7 days. If access is revoked through Google's security settings page, all stored YouTube data is deleted within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions or complaints, open an issue on this site's GitHub page.
